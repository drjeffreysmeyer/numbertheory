Proposition 5.1.8. Two is a quadratic residue modulo \(p\) when \(p \equiv \pm 1 \pmod{8}\).

If \(p\) is an odd prime, then \(2\) is a quadratic residue modulo \(p\) if and only if \(p \equiv \pm 1 \pmod{8}\text{.}\)
